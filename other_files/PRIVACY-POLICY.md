# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 2026-01-06  
**Last Updated:** 2026-01-06  

---

## 1. Information We Collect
We collect limited information necessary to provide, maintain, and improve our services in Groups Community. During email or Google login, we collect your **unique user ID, name, email address, and profile photo URL**. 

When a user shares a WhatsApp group, we collect the **group invite link, group name, assigned category, and group profile image** if publicly available. 

> **Important:** We strictly do not collect private messages, personal contacts, or users' phone numbers.

---

## 2. Why We Use Google / Email Login
We use Google or email-based login to:
* Securely store user data under a unique login ID.
* Prevent fake or duplicate accounts.
* Allow users to recover their shared groups data if they reinstall the app or change their device.
* Link shared WhatsApp groups to real user accounts for accountability, security, and moderation purposes.

**Note:** We do not access email inboxes, personal contacts, or private content.

---

## 3. Allowed Content and Group Categories
Groups Community is a platform intended for sharing safe, lawful, and community-driven WhatsApp groups. Users are permitted to share public groups related to constructive categories such as:
* Education & Technology
* Gaming & Entertainment
* Business & Finance
* Sports & Jobs

*All shared group links must be active, public, and accurately classified according to their actual content.*

---

## 4. WhatsApp Group Review and Publishing
When a user submits a WhatsApp group link, it is initially placed in a **Pending status**. Our moderation team reviews the link to ensure safety, validity, and appropriate classification before publishing it to the public directory. 

*We reserve the absolute right to reject, modify, or remove any group listing that violates our community standards, contains misleading descriptions, or breaches applicable laws.*

---

## 5. Prohibited Content and Account Banning
Sharing offensive, adult (18+), illegal, hate speech, violent, gambling, fraudulent, or malicious WhatsApp links is strictly prohibited. 

> **Warning:** If a user violates these policies, repeatedly submits spam links, or attempts to bypass our review system, we reserve the right to take immediate action. This includes deleting all associated group submissions and permanently banning the user's account from Groups Community without prior notice.

---

## 6. How We Use Your Data
User data is utilized for:
* User authentication and account management.
* Displaying user profile information within the app.
* Reviewing, validating, and publishing WhatsApp groups.
* Preventing spam and improving app performance.
* Maintaining security and compliance.

---

## 7. Data Storage and Security
We store user data securely using reasonable and industry-standard security measures. While we strive to protect user information, no digital storage or transmission method over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

---

## 8. Data Sharing
We **do not sell, rent, lease, or trade** user personal data. Data may only be shared:
1. If required by law.
2. To protect user safety.
3. With trusted service providers strictly for essential app functionality.

**User data is never shared with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.**

---

## 9. User Responsibilities
Users are solely responsible for ensuring that the WhatsApp groups they share are legal, safe, and do not violate any third-party rights or terms of service. Users must ensure that they have the right to share the public group invite link.

---

## 10. Data Retention and Deletion
User data is retained as long as the account remains active. If a user requests account deletion through the app's support system, the associated profile data and shared groups will be removed from our active databases within a reasonable timeframe, unless legally required to retain specific logs.

---

## 11. Children’s Privacy
This application is not intended for children under the age of **13**. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

---

## 12. Policy Updates
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our moderation policies or legal requirements. Significant changes will be communicated through the app or updated policy notices. Continued use of the app after updates constitutes acceptance of the modified policy.

---

## 13. Contact Information
If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, content guidelines, or a ban appeal, you can contact us through the **application’s support section**.
