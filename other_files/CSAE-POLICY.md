# Child Sexual Abuse & Exploitation (CSAE) Prevention Policy

**Effective Date:** June 4, 2026

Welcome to **Groups Community**. We are committed to providing a safe, clean, and helpful platform for our users to discover and share public group links. We maintain a strict zero-tolerance policy against any form of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) or Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE).

## 1. Prohibited Content and Conduct
Users, admins, and contributors are strictly prohibited from sharing, submitting, promoting, or linking to any content that involves, relates to, or depicts child sexual abuse, exploitation, or harm. This includes, but is not limited to, group invitation links that lead to illegal or harmful content.

## 2. Monitoring and Moderation Standards
To ensure compliance with Google Play Store policies and international legal standards, our moderation team regularly reviews user-generated content and submitted links. Any link or content found to be in violation of these safety standards is permanently and instantly deleted from our database without prior warning.

## 3. Reporting Mechanism
We encourage our community to report any suspicious or violating group links immediately. If you encounter any group link within our app that breaches this policy, please report it immediately by contacting us directly via email at: **[Aap Ka Developer Email Yahan Likhein]**

## 4. Strict Enforcement and Law Enforcement Cooperation
Any individual found violating these standards will face an immediate, lifetime ban from the Groups Community application. All related data will be wiped out. Furthermore, we reserve the right to cooperate fully with internet service providers and appropriate law enforcement authorities to report illegal activities.
